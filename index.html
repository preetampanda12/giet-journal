      From the Chief Editor

      “This journal is a place where ideas from our campus can reach readers across the world. We hope it inspires researchers in every field, and we invite you to share your work with us.”
      Dr. Jitendra KumarChief Editor, Department of ECE, GIET University

      As GIET University marks 25 years, the journal gives students, scholars and innovators a platform to publish and be read. Our thanks to the university’s leadership for their support.

      What we publish

      A multidisciplinary journal with four broad areas. Papers that cross these lines are welcome.

      Engineering

      Electronics, computing, AI, cybersecurity, IoT, civil, mechanical and electrical systems.

      Science

      Physics, chemistry, materials, environment, health sciences and pharmacy.

      Agriculture

      Crop science, soil health, agritech, food quality and sustainable farming.

      Management

      Finance, markets, business intelligence, entrepreneurship and policy.

      How to submit

      Submissions are made by email. Papers can be up to five pages, including references.

      Read the full guidelines
      1. Format your paper

        Use the journal’s Word template and keep it within five pages, with DOIs for references where available.

      2. Prepare a blind PDF

        Remove all author names and affiliations so the paper can be reviewed blind.

      3. Add your files

        Include an editable version in a ZIP file and a scan of the signed copyright form.

      4. Email the editors

        Send everything to journal@giet.edu with every co-author’s name, email and affiliation.
